# Final Recommendations Report — Musina 33/11 kV Substation
**Brownfield Protection Review**  
**Document ID:** MUS‑RPT‑008‑R0  
**Date:** 2026‑06‑12  

### Root Cause of the 51‑Minute Outage
Deficiencies D‑002 and D‑003: identical TMS settings on all feeder relays coupled with non‑directional instantaneous misapplication caused a fault on F3 to trip all healthy feeders. The hospital and water treatment plant lost supply because no grading existed.

### Immediate Actions (within 1 month)
- Apply graded TMS settings (D‑002) — ZAR 15 000.
- Disable non‑directional I>> (D‑003) — included.
- Verify F2 CT ratio (D‑001) — ZAR 5 000.

### Short‑Term Actions (3‑6 months)
- Install SEF protection (D‑004) — ZAR 47 000.
- Earth grid test and extension (D‑006) — ZAR 35 000.
- Replace 33 kV arresters (D‑007) — ZAR 12 000.
- Implement SSEG transfer‑trip (D‑009) — ZAR 45 000.
- Conductor verification (D‑010) — ZAR 3 000.

### Medium‑Term Actions (12 months)
- Transformer differential + REF (D‑005) — ZAR 180 000.
- SCADA upgrade to IEC 61850 (D‑008) — ZAR 85 000.

**Total estimated investment: ZAR 427 000** to bring the substation to full compliance and prevent a recurrence of the incident.
